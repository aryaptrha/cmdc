    02 / Machine selection

    Choosing a frame.

    Each frame is scored on contrast and edge density, then ranked. The score is a measure of how much is happening in the image, not how good it looks.

    03 / Fragment selection

    Cutting a rectangle out of the frame.

    The densest region of the winning frame is measured and a rectangle is drawn inside it. That rectangle is the fragment. No person has seen the frame it came from.
